# 🏎️ DHAKA CITY 3D (ঢাকা সিটি রেসার ৩ডি)

A full **true 3D** open-city racing and free-roam driving experience set across the multi-block streets, elevated flyovers, roundabouts, and alleys of **Dhaka, Bangladesh**!

---

## 🌟 What's New in This Version

### 1. Fully Responsive for All Devices (Mobile, Tablet, Desktop)
- **Fluid Multi-Device Layout**: Fully adaptive viewport with CSS `clamp()`, flexible UI cards, and notch safe-area insets (`env(safe-area-inset-top)`).
- **On-Screen Touch Controls**: Dedicated thumb-friendly touch buttons on touch devices and small viewports:
  - Steering (`◀` `▶`), Gas (`⚡ GAS`), Reverse/Brake (`🛑 REV`)
  - Drift (`💨 DRIFT`), Nitro Boost (`🔥 NITRO`), Horn (`📢 HONK`)
  - Camera switch button (`🎥 CAM`) to toggle Chase and Hood views instantly!

### 2. Hatirjheel Lake (হাতিরঝিল লেক - Inaccessible Scenic Zone)
- **Shimmering Water & Promenade**: Prominent lake in the Southeast city block with real-time shimmering wave animation, water fountains, and floating water lilies.
- **Iconic Hatirjheel Arched Bridge**: Tubular white cable-stayed arch bridge spanning across the water.
- **Protective Stone Promenade Railings**: Inaccessible stone boundary barriers prevent driving into the water; colliding with the railing triggers realistic water spray particles and splash sound effects before bouncing your vehicle safely back onto the road!

### 3. Dense Traffic & Diverse Bangladeshi Vehicles
- 🛵 **Delivery Motorbikes (Pathao & Foodpanda)**: Agile motorbikes with helmeted riders and branded delivery boxes weaving through traffic.
- 🚐 **Dhaka Laguna / Human Hauler (হিউম্যান হলার / লেগুনা)**: Iconic local shared microbuses with blue cabins and rear passenger canopies.
- 🛺 **Cycle Rickshaws (রিকশা)**: Authentic cycle rickshaws with passenger hood art and pedaling pullers with red gamchas.
- 🟢 **Auto Rickshaws (Green CNG Baby Taxis)**: 3-wheeled autorickshaws with yellow safety cages and sputtered engine audio.
- 🚌 **Dhaka Local Buses**: Colorful, heavy transit buses (*শিকড় পরিবহন, বলাকা এক্সপ্রেস, বিকল্প অটো*) with hydraulic air horns.
- 🚗 **Toyota Premio 1.5, Yellow Dhaka Cabs & VIP Black Prado SUVs**.

### 4. 3D Walking Pedestrians / Humans (পথচারী)
- **Animated Walk Cycles**: Dhakaiya pedestrians dressed in Panjabi, Sarees, Lungi, and modern streetwear walking along sidewalks and zebra crossings.
- **Honk Panic Dodge**: Pressing **`H`** or tapping the on-screen **`📢 HONK`** button makes nearby pedestrians panic and scramble to the outer curbs!
- **Dhakaiya Voice Lines**: Near-misses trigger humorous local dialogues:
  - *"এই ভাই দেখে চালান না! রাস্তা কি বাপের?!"*
  - *"আরে মামা ফুটপাতে গাড়ি উঠান ক্যান?!"*
  - *"সাবধান! পথচারী প্রায় চাপা পড়ছিলো!"*

### 5. Authentic Bangladeshi Architecture & Storefronts
- **Ground-Floor Storefront Signboards**: Authentic bilingual signs in Bengali and English:
  - *স্বপ্ন সুপারশপ (Shwapno Super Shop)*
  - *হাজী বিরিয়ানি অ্যান্ড রেস্তোরাঁ (Haji Biryani)*
  - *লাজ ফার্মা ও ফার্মেসী (Lazz Pharma & Medicine)*
  - *টং এর গরম চা ও নাস্তা (Dhaka Chai Corner)*
  - *রকমারি বুক কর্নার (Rokomari Books)*
  - *বিকাশ ও নগদ এজেন্ট (bKash & Nagad Agent)*
  - *আড়ং ঢাকা লাইফস্টাইল (Aarong Dhaka)*
  - *প্রাণ-আরএফএল ফ্রেশ মার্ট (PRAN-RFL Fresh Mart)*
- **Roadside Tong Tea Stalls (টং এর দোকান)**: Corner tea stalls with wooden benches, blue/red tarpaulins, aluminum kettles, and glass snack jars.
- **Balconies & Rooftop Gazi Tanks**: Multi-story residential and commercial buildings featuring front balconies with railings and iconic blue Gazi water tanks on rooftops.

### 6. Mass-Based Physics & Indestructible Free-Roam
- **Real Momentum Transfer**: Heavy buses (8,500 kg) rebound lighter cars, while heavy SUVs (2,600 kg) plow through lightweight rickshaws (120 kg).
- **Zero Game-Over Screen**: The car never dies! If your car takes heavy damage, floating **Repair Kits (🛠️)** restore it to 100% "Brand New", while **Chai Cups (☕)** refill health and nitro boost!

### 7. 100% Offline Three.js
- Bundled with a local `three.min.js` file — **no internet connection or CDN required**! Works seamlessly when opened via `file:///`.

## 🎮 Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Mobile / Touch Screen |
|---|---|---|
| **Accelerate Forward** | `▲` or `W` | `⚡ GAS` |
| **Brake / Reverse** | `▼` or `S` | `🛑 REV` |
| **Steer Left / Right** | `◀` `▶` or `A` `D` | `◀` `▶` |
| **Handbrake 3D Drift** | `SPACE` | `💨 DRIFT` |
| **Dhaka Horn (Clear Traffic!)** | `H` | `📢 HONK` |
| **CNG Nitro Turbo** | `SHIFT` or `N` | `🔥 NITRO` |
| **Toggle Camera (Chase / Hood)**| `C` | `🎥 CAM` |

1. **Double-click `play.bat`** to immediately launch the game in your default browser.
2. Or open `index.html` directly in Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox on any phone, tablet, or PC!
